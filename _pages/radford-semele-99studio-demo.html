Buttons and simple links

The most compatible route: ordinary school-website buttons linking to locked 99 Studio practice.

Buttons are the simplest option. The school website creates its own normal buttons and 99 Studio supplies the destination links. No custom HTML or iframe is required.

99 Club practice

All 14 Club levels can be linked separately, so parents choose exactly the level their child is working on.

Printable game sheets
