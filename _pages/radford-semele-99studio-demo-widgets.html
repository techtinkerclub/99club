Embedded widgets

The compact option: many school-selected choices presented inside two embedded panels.

Widgets are the compact multi-choice option. One embedded panel can hold many school-selected choices without filling the school page with dozens of separate links.

99 Club Widget

This example contains every Club level: 11–99 plus Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond.

Website setup: one Embed/HTML/Code block. The school chooses the visible levels and rules before copying the embed.

Maths Games Widget

Three printable puzzle-pack choices plus a small curated Play Online section. The tabs keep the two types of activity separate.

Website setup: a separate Games widget can be changed independently of the 99 Club widget. Printable packs still create fresh PDFs when parents download them.
