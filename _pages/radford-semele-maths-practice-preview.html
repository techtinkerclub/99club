What to test

  1. Open a 99 Club card and check the parent page looks simple and locked.
  2. Generate a new worksheet and matching answers.
  3. Open each printable puzzle pack and create a fresh pack.
  4. Try online games on both a phone and desktop.
  5. Use the browser Back button and make sure the journey still feels natural.
